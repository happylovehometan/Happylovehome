5. Healing Ethics and Participant Rights

Happy Love Home Healing respects each participant's autonomy, boundaries and privacy. Our courses, activities and healing services support self-awareness. We do not use fear, shame or claims of authority to pressure anyone to comply, purchase or become dependent.

Voluntary participation

You may decline to share personal experiences, answer uncomfortable questions or join an exercise. You may stop participating or decline further services, and seek another practitioner or qualified professional. Payments already made remain subject to the refund and rescheduling terms on this page. These choices will not be labelled resistance, low energy or a failure to grow.

Trauma and privacy

You do not have to disclose trauma, family matters or other sensitive experiences in a group, livestream or class. We will not use identifiable healing content, private conversations, images or stories in promotion without your clear consent. We seek consent before sharing feedback and aim to remove identifying and highly sensitive details.

Honest information and choice

Healing, meditation, Reiki, card readings, hypnosis and related experiences do not guarantee recovery from illness, resolution of mental health concerns, improved relationships, a partner, increased income or any other particular outcome. BoBo offers support and tools, without making life decisions for participants. We do not pressure anyone to renew services by claiming they will lose progress or energy, or require anyone to recruit others to receive services.

Professional boundaries and crisis referral

These services do not provide medical care, psychiatric treatment, psychotherapy or emergency crisis support. If you are thinking of harming yourself or another person, experiencing a serious mental health crisis, violence or an immediate threat to your safety, contact local emergency services, medical care, a qualified mental health professional or a crisis support service. In immediate danger, seek local emergency help now rather than waiting for BoBo to reply.

BoBo will not advise you to stop prescribed medication, replace medical care with energy healing, dismiss serious symptoms as an energy cleanse, or delay necessary professional help.

Healing should help you reclaim your ability to choose, rather than make you dependent on BoBo.
