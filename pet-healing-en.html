PET AND CAREGIVER

Your pet’s state may sometimes reflect the emotional atmosphere at home

Pets who live closely with us become familiar with our voice, body language, routine and emotional changes. A caregiver’s long-term stress, grief, fear or tension may sometimes affect the way a pet responds to the home environment.

This does not mean every difficulty is caused by the caregiver. Pain, illness, temperament, past experiences and environmental triggers may also play an important role.

Awareness is not about blame. It is an opportunity to care for both your pet and yourself.

Sometimes we begin by seeking help for our pet, and then realise that their condition has also brought our own anxiety, fear of loss, need for control or emotional exhaustion to the surface. Personal healing support for the caregiver can also be arranged with BoBo.
